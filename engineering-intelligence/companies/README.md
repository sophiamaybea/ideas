# Tech Company Money Map

The dashboard currently indexes 100 profitable technology-company records from a September 2026 dataset based on SEC EDGAR filings.

The first 20 have qualitative business-model profiles. The hourly process enriches at least 10 unprofiled companies per run until all indexed companies explain:
- what the company does;
- how it makes money;
- its primary profit engine;
- its moat;
- current growth drivers;
- material risks;
- transferable lessons for our own systems.

Financial ranking source:
https://www.companiesranked.com/en/most-profitable-technology-companies

Financial figures are evidence. Qualitative explanations are analysis and should remain distinguishable from filing data.
