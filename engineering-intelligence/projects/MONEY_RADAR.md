# Money Radar

## Definition
A real-time computational capital-allocation engine for scarce human/agent time.

Question:
> Given skills, tools, eligibility, time, risk tolerance and live opportunities, what action should be taken now to maximise expected monetary and reusable value?

## Pipeline
```
DISCOVER → NORMALISE → DEDUPLICATE → VERIFY → ENRICH
→ ESTIMATE → SCORE → OPTIMISE → SIMULATE → RANK → ACT → LEARN
```

## Canonical record
Separate:
- **observed** fields;
- **estimated** fields;
- **derived** fields;
- **provenance**;
- freshness;
- uncertainty.

## Decision model
Go beyond raw expected payout. Model:
- probability of eligibility;
- probability of completion;
- probability of acceptance/payment;
- stochastic time-to-complete;
- time-to-cash;
- direct cost;
- opportunity cost;
- competition;
- downside risk;
- reusable-work value;
- learning value;
- portfolio covariance;
- value of further information.

## Key inventions
- Money Velocity

- Reusable Work Graph
- Portfolio of compatible opportunities

## Product leap
The long-term category is closer to **fintech for human/agent time** than a job board: a Bloomberg-style terminal that prices possible actions and allocates effort.
