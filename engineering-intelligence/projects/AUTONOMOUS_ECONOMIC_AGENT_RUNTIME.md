# Autonomous Economic Agent Runtime — "The Foundry"

## Thesis
Build technology that can repeatedly turn evidence of unmet demand into bounded, testable, revenue-seeking autonomous operations.

It is not an AI agency. It is a runtime for:
1. discovering opportunities;
2. estimating their expected value;
3. identifying missing capabilities;
4. sourcing/building those capabilities;
5. spawning specialist workers;
6. running constrained experiments;
7. delivering work/products;
8. measuring actual revenue, cost and failure;
9. learning;
10. reallocating resources across ventures.

## Business genome
Every venture should be representable as data:
- customer;
- job-to-be-done;
- evidence;
- offer;
- distribution;
- price;
- acquisition method;
- delivery workflow;
- agent topology;
- tool/capability dependencies;
- variable/fixed costs;
- risk;
- experiments;
- results;
- reusable assets;
- confidence;
- status.

## Evolution
Variation → experiment → observed fitness → selection → mutation → inheritance.

"Fitness" is multi-objective: profit, speed-to-cash, learning, durability, risk, reuse and strategic option value.

## Safety boundary
Autonomy is permissioned. Financial transfers, credential changes, legal commitments, destructive operations and other consequential actions require explicit policy and, when appropriate, human approval.

## Near-term technical proof
The first valuable proof is not "run ten businesses". It is:
**one loop that discovers an opportunity, compiles a capability plan, executes a bounded task, records cost/outcome, and updates its decision policy.**
